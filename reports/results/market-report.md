# European Power Market Fundamentals — market report

DE-LU prices with German fundamentals and German physical border flows, full calendar 2024. Descriptive associations, not causal effects. Luxembourg is a German physical border here, not an external border of the DE-LU bidding zone.

Negative prices: 457.00 hours (5.20%). Mean daily spread: 111.20 EUR/MWh.
## Relationships
Price Spearman association: residual load 0.862; wind plus solar -0.688; net imports 0.549. Higher residual load coincided with higher prices, while more wind and solar coincided with lower prices. Imports also coincided with higher prices; this is consistent with multiple market responses and does not show that imports caused higher prices. Seasonal and hourly patterns are not controlled here.

## Decision relevance and limits
Negative-price exposure matters for renewable capture revenues; daily ranges help screen flexibility opportunities but are not achievable storage profit. Efficiency, capacity, cycling costs and dispatch constraints are absent. Actual generation and physical flows occur after the auction and cannot establish an actionable forecast. Physical flows differ from commercial schedules and include loop flows. Weather, outages, fuel and carbon prices, and neighbouring price differentials remain omitted. No causal or hedge recommendation follows from these associations. Negative episodes touching sample boundaries are censored.
